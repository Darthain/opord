# ATP 3-21.8 review notes for ARTIC AI v0.3.13

Source basis: Headquarters, Department of the Army, ATP 3-21.8, *Infantry Rifle Platoon and Squad*, 11 January 2024 including Change 1. The publication presents techniques as guides rather than prescriptive rules. ARTIC therefore adapts the command/control ideas to Arma rather than reproducing every U.S. Army drill literally.

## Concepts adopted

### Movement formations versus movement techniques
ATP distinguishes formation geometry from movement technique. Traveling is associated with contact not likely, traveling overwatch with contact possible, and bounding overwatch with contact expected. ARTIC v0.3.13 uses this distinction explicitly. For gameplay/cohesion, UAS knowledge of a future battlefield produces TRAVELLING_OVERWATCH rather than automatically starting fireteam bounds; bounding is reserved for the actual contact/tactical-play layer.

### Actions on contact
ARTIC now exposes the four-stage leader framework: REACT, DEVELOP THE SITUATION, CHOOSE AN ACTION, EXECUTE/REPORT. The existing first-contact LINE drill is the REACT/DEVELOP portion; the battlefield plan and tactical play are the CHOOSE/EXECUTE portion.

### Fireteam and squad control
The squad leader controls movement and distribution of fires through team leaders. A persistent base fireteam is therefore designated. When a standalone section attacks, the base fireteam is the first support/base-of-fire element and the other fireteam is the manoeuvre element when both remain viable.

### Support by fire and assault
The platoon chooses the general SBF area; the fixing section refines its exact terrain position. Assault sections remain covered/concealed until the support force has set the suppression condition. Final assault movement is synchronized through persistent assault positions and a final coordination line; support fires are then shifted as the assault closes.

### Control measures
The battlefield context now contains: release point, HOLD/attack position, probable line of deployment (PLD), SBF/FIRE line, assault position, final coordination line (FCL), limit of advance (LOA), lateral boundaries, reserve/rally areas and section fire sectors. These are planning controls, not arbitrary destinations for individual soldiers.

### Consolidation
A completed assault transitions to consolidation/reassessment rather than instantly generating another independent flank. Follow-on work will expand security, ammo/casualty accounting and reorganization.

## Important ARTIC adaptation
ATP allows bounding overwatch before direct contact when contact is expected. The current ARTIC test branch intentionally uses traveling overwatch during the longer pre-contact approach because our live tests showed early bounding amplified Arma pathfinding/cohesion failures. ARTIC can later reintroduce pre-contact bounding at short danger areas once the section/fireteam guide layer is proven stable.
